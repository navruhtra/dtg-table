# Sources of the test images

The 15 images here (04 to 18) are synthetic. American Dream Network
made them for the Separation Table with its
`scripts/make-test-images.py` (no third-party content), and they are
covered by the MIT License. They are the same files as in
https://github.com/navruhtra/separation-table/tree/00e00bb2b59efa600e859018d56f088b6f15361c/test-images

`cases.json` gives each image's garment colour and notes. The
benchmark (`benchmark/run.mjs`) and the browser test use them.
