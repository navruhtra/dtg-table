# DTG Table 0.1.0

The first public release of the DTG Table: direct-to-garment print
files, in the browser. It writes a colour layer and white layers
for your printer's own software; it does not drive a printer.

- White where the colour needs it, by garment: none on white
  garments, full white under every colour by default, and least
  white (with the colour file compensated) once you import a
  measured white ramp.
- Garment knockout: where the art is the shirt's own colour, no ink
  and no white.
- Highlight white under near-white art, white density and choke.
- Printer presets with cited sources (Epson SureColor F2270 and
  F1070, Brother GTXpro, Kornit Atlas MAX Plus, or your own), and 18
  garment colours or your own.
- Colour and white files as PNG or TIFF at full resolution, with the
  print resolution in the file, plus a placement sheet.
- A garment proof with ΔE00 against the art (model only).
- Nothing is uploaded: the art stays in your browser tab.

## Use it

Open
https://americandreamnetwork.us/tools/printing/dtg-printing/dtg-table
in a browser. Nothing to install.

To run it yourself: `npm run setup`, then `npm start` (README,
Run it locally).

## Checks in this release

- Unit and property tests for the white rule, knockout, presets,
  layer files, the proof, measured ramps and placement.
- The benchmark gate against `benchmark/baseline.json` on 15
  synthetic test images.
- A browser test of every function of the page at 390 × 844 and
  1280 × 800, alone and framed as on the ADN site.

## Checksums

`src/vendor.sha256` holds the SHA-256 of every file `npm run setup`
downloads, as pinned in `scripts/vendor-pins.mjs`, including the
Separation Table engine at commit 00e00bb. Check a copy with
`sha256sum -c vendor.sha256` in `src/`, or
`node scripts/notices.mjs --check`. Third-party licenses:
THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md.

## Known limits

- Every quality number is a model number until the test targets are
  printed and measured.
- Whether a RIP keeps a white file you supply is not checked yet.
- White as a channel inside one TIFF is not written yet; white goes
  as separate files.
